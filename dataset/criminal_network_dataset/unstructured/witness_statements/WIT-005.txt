WITNESS STATEMENT

Case: FIR-2024-005 (Money Laundering)
Date of Statement: 8th April 2024
Recorded by: ACP Bangalore / Goa Police
Witness: Conference Room Attendant, Taj Fort Aguada Resort, Goa (Name withheld)

STATEMENT:

I work in the banquets and conferences department at Taj Fort Aguada Resort, Goa.

On 15th March 2024, Conference Room B (smaller executive room) was booked from 11 AM to 2 PM under the name "Corporate Solutions - Bangalore". The booking was made by a lady named Priya Sharma - she called personally to arrange everything.

The attendees arrived as follows:
1. Priya Sharma - arrived around 10:45 AM in a silver Mercedes (KA-01-UV-1234). She went straight to the conference room.
2. Sunil Verma - arrived around 11:00 AM in a black BMW X5 (MH-04-ST-7890).
3. Kiran Desai - arrived around 11:10 AM in a blue Ford Endeavour (GA-07-NP-9012).
4. A foreign gentleman - arrived around 11:15 AM in a black sedan with a CD number plate (diplomatic). He was met at the main gate by our security and escorted directly to the conference room. He looked European, maybe 50s, wearing a dark suit.

I served tea and coffee to the room at 11:30 AM and 12:30 PM. When I entered:
- Priya Sharma was standing at the head of the table, speaking. The others were listening and taking notes.
- There were laptops open, documents spread on the table.
- The foreign gentleman spoke in English with an accent. Priya Sharma replied in English.
- Sunil Verma and Kiran Desai mostly listened, occasionally asked questions in Hindi.

At 12:30 PM when I served coffee, the foreign gentleman was showing something on his phone to Priya Sharma. She took a photo of his screen with her phone.

The meeting ended around 2:00 PM. The foreign gentleman left first in his diplomatic car. Priya Sharma, Sunil Verma, and Kiran Desai went to our restaurant for lunch. They stayed until about 3:30 PM, continuing their discussion.

Priya Sharma paid the conference room bill (Rs. 45,000) by card - corporate card of "Tech Solutions Pvt Ltd".

I remember Priya Sharma specifically because she booked the room again for August 15th week - said "quarterly review meeting".

The diplomatic car was unusual - in my 8 years here, I've seen diplomatic plates maybe 3-4 times. Our security logs will have the vehicle number.
