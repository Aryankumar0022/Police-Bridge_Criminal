ASSESSMENT:
This is a textbook multi-layered laundering operation with professional structuring (shell companies, offshore, properties, crypto, gold, diplomatic cover). The use of diplomatic vehicles for coordination meetings represents a significant escalation suggesting possible state-level facilitation or exploitation of diplomatic privileges. The fusion of drug, fraud, and arms proceeds through a single architecture (Tech Solutions/Oceanic Holdings) creates a "super-node" whose disruption would cascade across all predicate offenses.

RECOMMENDATIONS:
1. Immediate PMLA attachment of all identified properties and bank accounts
2. Provisional arrest of Priya Sharma (architect), Kiran Desai (property front), Sunil Verma (fraud source), Rajesh Kumar (drug source)
