WITNESS STATEMENT

Case: FIR-2024-005 (Money Laundering)
Date of Statement: 12th April 2024
Recorded by: ACP Bangalore / Goa Police
Witness: Property Broker, Calangute, Goa (Name withheld)

STATEMENT:

I'm a property broker in Calangute, Goa. I've been in this business for 15 years.

In January 2024, a lady named Kiran Desai approached me through a reference. She wanted to buy a villa in Calangute for "her boss, Priya Sharma from Bangalore". She said budget 75-80 lakhs.

I showed her 3 properties. She selected a villa near Baga River - 3 BHK, pool, 500 sqm plot. Owner was an NRI from UK wanting quick sale.

Deal closed at Rs. 75 lakhs. Registration on 25th January 2024. Buyer: Kiran Desai (PAN provided). Payment: RTGS from ICICI Bank account (later traced to Priya Sharma's account).

Kiran Desai said "Madam (Priya) will come for possession". Priya Sharma came on 28th January - arrived in a Mercedes (KA-01-UV-1234). She inspected the villa, seemed satisfied. Stayed 2 hours. Left for Taj Hotel.

Then in February, Kiran Desai came back - said "Madam wants to sell, got better property in Bangalore". Listed at Rs. 1.1 Crore. Sold to a Delhi buyer (cash + loan) on 10th February 2024 for Rs. 1 Crore. Registration same day. Seller: Kiran Desai. Payment: RTGS to Priya Sharma's ICICI account.

Profit: Rs. 25 lakhs in 16 days. In my 15 years, I've never seen a flip this fast at this margin in Calangute. The market doesn't move that fast.

Kiran Desai also asked about commercial property in Panaji - "for a hospitality venture". I showed her a few. She said "Madam will decide after Goa meeting in March".

The March meeting - Priya Sharma, Sunil Verma (Mumbai), Kiran Desai, and a foreign gentleman at Taj Hotel. I know because Kiran Desai booked my client's villa for their stay (Taj Fort Aguada). The foreign gentleman had a diplomatic car - I saw it at the hotel entrance.

Kiran Desai's Ford Endeavour (GA-07-NP-9012) - I've seen it many times. She drives it herself sometimes.

Priya Sharma - sharp, business-like, speaks English mostly. Sunil Verma - quieter, follows Priya's lead. Kiran Desai - efficient, handles paperwork.

The villa flip - 75L to 1Cr in 16 days - is the biggest red flag. No renovation, no value add. Pure paper profit. The money trail: Priya -> Kiran (75L) -> Priya (1Cr). Circular.

That's my statement.
