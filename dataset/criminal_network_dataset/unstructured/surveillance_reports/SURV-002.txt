OBSERVATIONS:

08:00 hrs - Surveillance team positioned near Golden Temple Road, Amritsar.

08:30 hrs - Subject Vikram Singh observed arriving in a black Tata Harrier (PB-10-GH-7890) at a tea stall near Golden Temple complex. Subject alone.

09:00 hrs - A Mahindra Scorpio bearing registration HR-26-DK-9876 arrived. Driver identified as Ravi Shankar (known associate from Lucknow). Passenger identified as Baldev Raj (arms supplier from Munger).

09:15 hrs - Vikram Singh, Ravi Shankar, and Baldev Raj met near the Scorpio. Observed loading 4-5 cardboard boxes from Scorpio into the trunk of Vikram's Tata Harrier. Boxes appeared heavy. Loading took approximately 20 minutes.

09:35 hrs - All three individuals engaged in conversation for approximately 15 minutes. Vikram Singh made three phone calls during this period (numbers not captured).

09:50 hrs - Baldev Raj and Ravi Shankar departed in Scorpio HR-26-DK-9876 heading towards NH44 (Delhi direction). Vikram Singh departed in Tata Harrier towards Amritsar city.

10:00 hrs to 14:00 hrs - Vikram Singh observed at his residence/office in Amritsar. No further significant movement.

VEHICLES OBSERVED:
- Tata Harrier PB-10-GH-7890 (Vikram Singh)
- Mahindra Scorpio HR-26-DK-9876 (Ravi Shankar / Baldev Raj)
- Toyota Innova DL-1C-MN-4567 (not seen today, but known to be in network)

NOTES:
- The box transfer from Scorpio to Tata Harrier is highly suspicious - consistent with arms/ammunition or cash movement.
- Baldev Raj (Munger arms supplier) and Ravi Shankar (transporter) physically meeting Vikram Singh in Amritsar confirms the supply chain.
- Three phone calls by Vikram Singh immediately after loading - likely to Mohammed Farooq (hawala), Rajesh Kumar (drug supply), and/or Priya Sharma (shell company).
- Scorpio HR-26-DK-9876 and Innova DL-1C-MN-4567 both linked to Ravi Shankar and Mohammed Farooq.
- This surveillance directly correlates with the arms interception in Lucknow (FIR-2024-004) approximately 7 weeks later.
