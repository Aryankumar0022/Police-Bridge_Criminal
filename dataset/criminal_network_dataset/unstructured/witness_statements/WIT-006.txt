Witness: Resident, House No. 43, Sector 12, Dwarka, Delhi (Name withheld)

STATEMENT:

I live two houses down from Rajesh Kumar (House No. 45, Sector 12, Dwarka). I've known him for about 3 years - he keeps to himself mostly, but we exchange greetings.

Things I've noticed over the past year:

1. The white Toyota Fortuner (DL-5C-AB-1234) - it comes and goes at odd hours. Often leaves around 10-11 PM, comes back early morning 4-5 AM. Sometimes gone for 2-3 days.

2. Visitors - not many, but the ones who come are distinctive:
   - A Sardar ji (turban, beard) - comes in a black SUV (Tata Harrier maybe). Comes once every 2-3 weeks. Stays 30-45 minutes. They talk in the garage, not inside the house.
   - A lady - comes in a taxi or sometimes a nice car (Mercedes?). Comes once a month maybe. She's well-dressed, carries a laptop bag. Rajesh's wife (if she is his wife) doesn't seem to like her visits.
   - A couple of other men - ordinary looking, but they come in expensive cars (BMW, Audi). Short visits, 15-20 minutes.

3. The house - they had some renovation work about 6 months ago. Workers were there for 2 weeks. Since then, the garage has a new heavy door with electronic lock. The basement windows were bricked up.

4. Noise - sometimes late at night, hear vehicles starting/stopping. Not loud parties or anything, just... business-like.

5. On 14th January (day before raid), I saw the Sardar ji's black SUV parked outside around 7 PM. Then the Fortuner left around 10 PM. The Sardar ji stayed till about 11 PM.

6. The lady (Priya Sharma - I heard the name once when she was on phone near the gate) - I've seen her 4-5 times in past 6 months. Always the same pattern - taxi, laptop bag, 1 hour, leaves.

I didn't think it was drugs - just thought maybe he's in some import-export business with the odd hours and visitors. But the police raid... now it makes sense.

The Sardar ji - I think his name is Vikram something. The lady is definitely Priya Sharma.

That's all.
