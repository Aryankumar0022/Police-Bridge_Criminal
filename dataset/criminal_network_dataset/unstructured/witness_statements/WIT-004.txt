WITNESS STATEMENT

Case: FIR-2024-004 (Arms Smuggling)
Date of Statement: 28th March 2024
Recorded by: SI Verma, Lucknow PS
Witness: Owner of "Singh Da Dhaba" on NH44 near Ambala, Haryana (Name withheld)

STATEMENT:

I run a dhaba on NH44 near Ambala. Truck drivers stop here regularly for food and rest.

Around 20th March 2024, a big truck (UP-32-BN-5678) stopped at my dhaba around 10:30 AM. The driver was a middle-aged man, looked like from UP/Bihar. He ordered tea and paratha.

While eating, he got a phone call. He spoke in Hindi with a Bihari accent. I heard him say "Baldev ji", "Amritsar pahunch jayenge", "Vikram bhai ko bol diya". He also said "Scorpio saath hai" and "Innova backup mein hai".

After the call, he seemed relaxed. He finished eating, paid cash, and left towards Amritsar.

A Mahindra Scorpio (HR-26-DK-9876) passed my dhaba about 15 minutes before the truck. And a Toyota Innova (DL-1C-MN-4567) passed about 10 minutes after the truck. Both going same direction - towards Amritsar.

I've seen that truck (UP-32-BN-5678) before - maybe 4-5 times in the last 3 months. Same driver usually. He always stops for tea around this time. He always has a Scorpio and/or Innova with him.

Once, around February, the driver was talking to another trucker at my dhaba. I heard "Munger se maal aaya hai", "Baldev ji ka kaam hai", "Punjab side jaana hai". The other trucker asked "Kya maal?" and he just smiled and said "Samajh jao".

I didn't think much of it - truckers talk in code. But when police came asking about the truck yesterday, I remembered.

The driver's face - medium build, moustache, roughly 35-40 years. Wears a turban sometimes, sometimes not.

That's all I know.
