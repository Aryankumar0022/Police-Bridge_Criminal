SUMMARY:
Intelligence indicates Vikram Singh (Amritsar) is expanding his criminal hub to integrate drug distribution, arms reception, and hawala coordination. The network shows increased activity on the Amritsar-Srinagar corridor suggesting deeper J&K linkages.

KEY FINDINGS:

1. NETWORK EXPANSION:

   - Storage facilities: 3 identified (Ranjit Avenue, Verka, Attari border area)
   - Vehicle fleet: Tata Harrier PB-10-GH-7890, Fortuner PB-08-WX-5678, Hyundai Tucson PB-02-MN-7890

2. KASHMIR CORRIDOR ACTIVITY:
   - GPS data (Ravi Shankar's truck) shows 5 trips to Srinagar/Jammu in 90 days
   - Vikram Singh's phone (9123456789) shows calls to 4 Kashmir-based numbers (not in CDR database)
   - Intelligence suggests arms diversion to militant groups in South Kashmir (Shopian, Pulwama)
   - Drug supply to Kashmir valley operators (heroin demand high in valley)

3. HAWALA INTEGRATION:
   - Daily coordination with Mohammed Farooq (Delhi) - morning calls 1000-1100 hrs
   - Settlement via AXIS 3333... to Desert Gold (Dubai) -> Emirates NBD
   - Vikram's PNB account receives hawala credits from Farooq's shells

4. DRUG-ARMS CONVERGENCE:
   - Same transporter (Ravi Shankar) for both heroin (Fortuner) and arms (Truck)
   - Same hawala operator (Farooq) for both proceeds and payments
   - Same front company (Northern Trading) for both drug and arms finance
   - Baldev Raj (Munger) supplies arms; Vikram distributes/sells onwards

5. COMMUNICATION SECURITY:

   - Signal/Telegram for sensitive comms
   - Face-to-face meetings at Golden Temple Road tea stalls (countersurveillance)
   - Avoids fixed lines; all mobile

6. FINANCIAL PROFILE:
   - Monthly drug revenue: Rs. 40-60 lakhs (estimated)
   - Monthly arms margin: Rs. 10-15 lakhs
   - Hawala commissions paid: Rs. 5-8 lakhs/month to Farooq

ASSESSMENT:
Vikram Singh has evolved from a drug distributor to a regional crime hub operator integrating narcotics, arms, and money movement. The Kashmir corridor activity is the most concerning - if arms are reaching militant groups, this escalates to national security threat (UAPA/NSA territory). The use of the same logistics (Ravi Shankar) and finance (Farooq/Northern Trading) for both drug and arms confirms a unified enterprise.

RECOMMENDATIONS:
1. Enhanced surveillance on Vikram Singh (Amritsar) - physical and technical
2. Intercept Kashmir-bound communications - coordinate with J&K Police/IB
3. Target Ravi Shankar's Srinagar/Jammu delivery points
4. Financial investigation of Vikram's PNB account and linked shells
5. Monitor Amritsar storage facilities
6. Consider NSA detention for Vikram Singh if Kashmir militant link confirmed
7. Joint Punjab-UP-Delhi operation for Ravi Shankar/Baldev Raj/Farooq nexus
