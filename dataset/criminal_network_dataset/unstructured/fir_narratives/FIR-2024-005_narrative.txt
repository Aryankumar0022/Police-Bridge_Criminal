FIRST INFORMATION REPORT NARRATIVE

On 5th April 2024, based on a Suspicious Transaction Report (STR) filed by the Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU-IND) regarding Tech Solutions Pvt Ltd (123 MG Road, Bangalore), a team led by ACP Bangalore initiated an investigation into suspected money laundering through shell companies.

The investigation revealed a complex web of shell companies and financial transactions estimated at over Rs. 75 crores, connecting multiple criminal networks across India and offshore jurisdictions.

Key findings from the investigation:

1. TECH SOLUTIONS PVT LTD (Bangalore)
   - Registered address: 123 MG Road, Bangalore
   - Director: Priya Sharma (also director of multiple shell entities)
   - Flagged by FIU for: High-value RTGS/NEFT transactions with no apparent business rationale, frequent cash deposits/withdrawals, transactions with offshore entities
   - Bank accounts: ICICI Bank (11111111111111, 99999999999999), HDFC Bank (33333333333333)

2. PRIYA SHARMA - Central figure in shell company operations
   - Linked to: Tech Solutions Pvt Ltd, Oceanic Holdings Ltd (Mauritius), Bangalore Realty Group, Goa Hospitality Ventures
   - Bank accounts: ICICI 77777777777777, HDFC 44444444444444, HDFC 88888888888888
   - Property purchases in Koramangala (Bangalore) and Calangute (Goa) through fronts

3. SUNIL VERMA - Fund source from fraud operations
   - Mumbai fraud network (FIR-2024-002) proceeds routed through Tech Solutions
   - ICICI account 55555555555555 transferred Rs. 50 lakhs to Tech Solutions (TXN016, TXN043, TXN071)
   - Crypto conversion pipeline via Deepa Nair and shell company accounts

4. RAJESH KUMAR - Drug proceeds source
   - HDFC account 12345678901234 transferred Rs. 15 lakhs to offshore shell (HDFC 99999999999999) which funded Tech Solutions (TXN031, TXN032)
   - Drug trafficking network (FIR-2024-001) proceeds laundered through this structure

5. KIRAN DESAI - Property front
   - HDFC account 88888888888888 used for property transactions
   - Goa property (Calangute) purchased for Rs. 75 lakhs (TXN009), sold for Rs. 1 crore (TXN014)

6. DEEPA NAIR - Fund router
