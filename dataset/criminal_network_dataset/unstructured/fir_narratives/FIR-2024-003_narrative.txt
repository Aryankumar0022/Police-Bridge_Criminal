FIRST INFORMATION REPORT NARRATIVE

On 10th March 2024, based on sustained surveillance and financial intelligence developed over 8 months, a team led by Inspector Singh conducted a raid at Shop No. 78, Chandni Chowk, Delhi, operated by Mohammed Farooq (approx. 45 years), resident of Old Delhi.

The raid uncovered a full-fledged illegal money transfer (hawala) operation. The following was seized:
- Handwritten ledgers (hawala registers) covering January 2022 to March 2024
- Cash amounting to Rs. 45,00,000/- in Indian and foreign currency
- Two vehicles: Mahindra Scorpio HR-26-DK-9876 and Toyota Innova DL-1C-MN-4567
- 4 mobile phones including number 9111222333 (Mohammed Farooq) and +971-50-1234567 (Hassan Ali, Dubai)
- A diary containing codes for international settlements

Analysis of the seized ledgers indicates a turnover exceeding Rs. 100 crores over the past two years. Key clients and transactions identified:

1. Vikram Singh (Amritsar) - Approximately Rs. 15 crores sent through the network for drug proceeds settlement and arms procurement payments. Regular client since mid-2022.

2. Deepa Nair (Kochi) - Approximately Rs. 8 crores routed through the channel for crypto conversion proceeds from Mumbai fraud operations (linked to FIR-2024-002).

3. Ravi Shankar (Lucknow) - Identified as primary cash transporter. Used Scorpio HR-26-DK-9876 and Innova DL-1C-MN-4567 for cash movement between Delhi, Amritsar, Lucknow, and Chandni Chowk. GPS data from vehicles confirms routes.

4. Amit Patel (Surat) - Received approximately Rs. 3 crores for gold purchases on behalf of the network. Gold sourced from Surat diamond market contacts.

5. Priya Sharma (Bangalore) - Visited Shop No. 78 at least 3 times in past 6 months (CCTV footage available). Believed to be coordinating shell company structures for layering.

6. Hassan Ali (Dubai) - International hawala contact. Phone +971-50-1234567. Frequent communication with Mohammed Farooq (87 calls in past 90 days). Settlements via Emirates NBD Bank, Dubai.
