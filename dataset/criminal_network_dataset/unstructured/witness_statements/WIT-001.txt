WITNESS STATEMENT

Case: FIR-2024-001 (Drug Trafficking)
Date of Statement: 16th January 2024
Recorded by: SI Verma, Rajouri Garden PS
Witness: Coffee Shop Owner, Karol Bagh Market, Delhi (Name withheld for protection)

STATEMENT:

I have been running a small coffee shop in Karol Bagh market for the past 12 years. On 14th January 2024, around 11:00 AM, a man who I now know as Rajesh Kumar came to my shop. He was driving a white Toyota Fortuner (I noted the number DL-5C-AB-1234 because it was a nice SUV).

He met a lady who comes to my shop sometimes - her name is Priya Sharma. She comes about once a month, usually alone, orders black coffee. That day she was waiting for him at a corner table.

They talked for about 45 minutes. I was behind the counter but could see them. They looked serious, not like friends chatting. At one point, Rajesh Kumar took out a brown envelope from his jacket and slid it across the table to Priya Sharma. She put it in her bag quickly. They both kept looking around, like they were watching for someone.

After they finished, Rajesh Kumar left in his Fortuner towards Chandni Chowk direction. Priya Sharma left about 10 minutes later in an auto-rickshaw.

I didn't think much of it at the time - just business people meeting. But when the police came asking about Rajesh Kumar yesterday, I remembered this meeting clearly. The brown envelope exchange was unusual - not something normal customers do.

I have seen Priya Sharma with another man also - a heavy-set guy, maybe 40s - at my shop about 3 months ago. They were discussing something about "property in Goa" and "Bangalore office". I didn't catch the name.

That's all I remember.
